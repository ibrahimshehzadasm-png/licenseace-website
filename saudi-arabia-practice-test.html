About this Saudi Arabia practice test

This free practice test is built from LicenseAce's Saudi Arabia question bank — 420 original questions written by our team and checked against the Saudi traffic regulations published by Moroor, the General Directorate of Traffic. They are practice questions written to the real test's syllabus and style, not copies of the official exam, and every answer carries a short explanation, so you learn the rule behind it rather than memorising a letter.

How to prepare with it

Work through the questions in short daily sessions, and read the explanation for every mistake — the questions you get wrong are the ones the real test is most likely to catch you on. When your scores are consistently strong, move to our traffic signs page and learn every sign you still hesitate on, then read the fees and guide page so the process, the cost and the documents hold no surprises on the day. The full picture for the region is on the Saudi Arabia hub page.
