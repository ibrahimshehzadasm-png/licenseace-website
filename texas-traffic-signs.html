Regulatory signs — 33 signs

Stop

Regulatory R1-1

Come to a complete stop at the stop line, crosswalk, or before the intersection; yield the right-of-way, then proceed when safe. Slowing down is not enough.

Yield

Regulatory R1-2

Slow down or stop as needed and yield the right-of-way to vehicles, pedestrians, and bicycles on the road you are joining or crossing.

Do Not Enter

Regulatory R5-1

The road ahead is one-way in the opposite direction. Do not drive into it.

Wrong Way

Regulatory R5-1a

You are travelling against traffic on a one-way street or ramp. Turn around or leave the roadway as soon as it is safe.

No Left Turn

Regulatory R3-2

Left turns are prohibited at this intersection.

No U-Turn

Regulatory R3-4

U-turns are prohibited here.

Turn Only

Regulatory R3-6

Traffic in this lane must turn in the direction of the arrow — going straight is not permitted from this lane. Where two lanes may turn left, the left lane must turn left.

Right Lane Must Turn Right

Regulatory R3-7

Traffic in the right lane must turn right — it may not go straight.

Do Not Pass

Regulatory R4-1

Passing another vehicle is prohibited here — do not cross the yellow line to pass.

Slower Traffic Keep Right

Regulatory R4-3

If you are driving slower than other traffic, keep in the right-hand lane.

Keep Right

Regulatory R4-7

Drive to the right of the traffic island, median, or obstruction ahead.

Speed Limit

Regulatory R2-1

The maximum speed in miles per hour under ideal conditions. The same limit applies day and night.

No Trucks

Regulatory R5-2

Trucks are prohibited from this roadway.

Disabled Parking

Regulatory R7-8

This space is reserved for vehicles displaying a disabled license plate or a disabled parking ID card. Do not park, stop, or stand here without one.

HOV Lane

Regulatory R3-14

This preferential lane is reserved for high-occupancy vehicles — buses and carpools meeting the occupancy shown — during the hours posted.

All Way

Regulatory R1-3P

Plaque under a STOP sign: traffic from all approaches must stop. The first vehicle to arrive goes first; if two arrive together, yield to the vehicle on your right.

Photo Enforced

Regulatory R10-18

Red-light violations at this intersection are enforced by camera (photo enforcement).

Road Closed

Regulatory R11-2

The road ahead is closed to all traffic. Do not enter — follow the detour.

Center Lane — Left Turns Only

Regulatory R3-9a

The center lane is reserved for left turns only, from both directions. Never use it for passing or through travel.

Left Lane for Passing Only

Regulatory R4-2aT

The far-left lane on this roadway is for passing only. Slower traffic must not travel in it.

Move Over or Slow Down

Regulatory -

Texas law: when an emergency vehicle, tow truck, utility vehicle, or TxDOT vehicle is stopped on the shoulder with its lights flashing, move over a lane — or slow down well below the speed limit if you cannot move over.

No Right Turn

Regulatory R3-1

Right turns are prohibited at this intersection.

No Turns

Regulatory R3-3

No turns of any kind are permitted at this intersection.

One Way

Regulatory R6-1

Traffic on this street moves in the direction of the arrow only.

Divided Highway Crossing

Regulatory R6-3

You are crossing a divided highway — cross the first roadway, stop in the median if needed, then cross the second roadway.

No Parking Any Time

Regulatory R7-1

Parking is prohibited at all times along this section of roadway.

No Parking

Regulatory R8-3

Parking is prohibited here (symbol sign).

Do Not Stop on Tracks

Regulatory R8-8

Never stop your vehicle on the railroad tracks.

Yield Here to Pedestrians

Regulatory R1-5

Stop or yield for pedestrians at the marked yield line ahead of the crosswalk.

In-Street Pedestrian Crossing

Regulatory R1-6

Yield to pedestrians in this crosswalk — the sign stands in the street at the crossing.

Minimum Speed Limit

Regulatory R2-4

Where this limit is posted, do not drive slower than the minimum speed shown unless conditions require it.

Bicycles May Use Full Lane

Regulatory R4-11

Bicycles may use the full travel lane on this roadway — do not expect them to stay at the edge.

Stop Here on Red

Regulatory R10-6

Stop at this point (line) when the traffic signal is red.

Warning signs — 57 signs

Signal Ahead

Warning W3-3

A traffic signal is ahead. Be prepared to slow down or stop.

Sharp Turn

Warning W1-1

The road turns sharply ahead (in the direction of the arrow). Slow down and keep to the right.

Low Clearance

Warning W12-2

The overpass or structure ahead has the vertical clearance shown. Do not enter if your load is higher.

Winding Road

Warning W1-5

The road ahead winds with a series of turns or curves. Slow down and drive carefully.

Divided Highway Begins

Warning W6-1

The highway ahead is divided by a median island. Keep to the right of the median.

Divided Highway Ends

Warning W6-2

The divided highway ends ahead and the road becomes a two-way roadway.

Added Lane

Warning W4-3

An additional lane begins ahead and joins your roadway; merging is not required.

Slippery When Wet

Warning W8-5

The pavement ahead is slippery when wet. Slow down and avoid sudden moves.

Reverse Turn

Warning W1-3

The road ahead bends first one way and then the other (reverse turn). Slow down.

Two-Way Traffic

Warning W6-3

The road ahead carries traffic in both directions. Keep right and expect oncoming traffic in the left lane.

Side Road

Warning W2-2

A side road joins your road ahead. Watch for traffic entering or crossing.

Curve

Warning W1-2

A curve is ahead (in the direction of the arrow). Slow down before you reach it.

Cross Road

Warning W2-1

A road crosses yours ahead. Watch right and left for cross traffic.

Lane Ends

Warning W4-2

One lane ends ahead and traffic must merge into the remaining lane (lane reduction).

T Intersection

Warning W2-4

Your road ends at a T intersection ahead. You must turn left or right; be prepared to yield.

Y Intersection

Warning W2-5

The road splits into a Y ahead. Be prepared for traffic from the side road.

Dip

Warning W8-2

There is a low place (dip) in the road ahead. Slow down.

Soft Shoulder

Warning W8-4

The shoulder ahead is soft. Stay on the pavement and avoid the shoulder.

Merging Traffic

Warning W4-1

Traffic from another lane or roadway merges into yours ahead — lanes come together.

Pedestrian Crossing

Warning W11-2

A pedestrian crossing area is ahead. Slow down and yield to pedestrians.

Narrow Bridge

Warning W5-2

The bridge ahead is narrow. Slow down, stay in your lane, and do not pass on the bridge.

Loose Gravel

Warning W8-7

Loose gravel covers the road ahead. Slow down — the surface is loose.

Reverse Curve

Warning W1-4

The road curves one way and then the other ahead (reverse curve). Slow down.

Object Marker

Warning OM1-1

An obstruction is in or close to the roadway ahead (such as a culvert or center pier). The marker shows where the obstruction is — pass with care.

Chevron Alignment

Warning W1-8

A sharp change in the road's direction is ahead. The chevrons point the way the road goes — slow down, especially at night.

Double Arrow

Warning W1-7

The road ends or is blocked ahead at an island or obstruction, but you may pass on either side of it.

Hill

Warning W7-1

A downgrade (hill) is ahead. Shift to a lower gear to help control speed.

Truck Crossing

Warning W8-6

Trucks cross or enter the road ahead. Slow down and watch for large vehicles.

Bump

Warning W8-1

There is a sudden high place (bump) in the road ahead. Slow down.

Road Narrows

Warning W5-1

The pavement narrows ahead. Slow down and stay in your lane.

Pavement Ends

Warning W8-3

The hard-surfaced pavement ends ahead and the road continues as an earth or low-type surface.

Low Shoulder

Warning W8-9

The shoulder ahead is lower than the pavement. Keep your wheels on the pavement.

Shoulder Drop-Off

Warning W8-17

The edge of the pavement drops off sharply to the shoulder ahead. Avoid dropping a wheel off the edge.

Stop Ahead

Warning W3-1

A stop sign is ahead. Slow down and be ready to stop.

Animal Crossing

Warning W11-4

Animals may cross the road ahead. Slow down and be prepared to stop if animals appear.

Disabled Crossing

Warning W11-9

A crossing used by disabled persons is ahead. Slow down and watch for wheelchair users and others crossing.

Rough Road

Warning W8-8

The road surface ahead is in poor condition (rough road). Slow down.

Ice on Bridge

Warning W8-13

The bridge ahead may be icy in cold weather — bridges ice over before other road surfaces. The sign may stay posted all winter.

Ramp Meter Ahead

Warning -

A freeway entrance ramp ahead is metered. When the yellow lights at the top and bottom of the sign flash, the ramp meter is operating — stop at the meter and enter one car at a time when it turns green.

Grooved Pavement

Warning W8-15

The pavement ahead is grooved. Drive with care — motorcyclists should use extra caution.

Object Marker — Bridge Rail End

Warning OM3-L

Marks the end of a bridge railing or a narrow-bridge rail end. Pass the marked end with care.

No Passing Zone

Warning W14-3

Advance warning of a no-passing zone: do not cross the yellow lines to pass.

Advisory Speed

Warning W13-1

The highest safe speed for the curve or condition ahead, in miles per hour. It advises — slow to this speed.

Exit Speed Advisory

Warning W13-3

The highest safe speed, in miles per hour, for the exit ramp ahead.

Side Road (Angle)

Warning W2-3

A side road joins your road at an angle ahead. Watch for entering traffic.

Circular Intersection

Warning W2-6

A roundabout (circular intersection) is ahead. Slow down and yield before entering.

Yield Ahead

Warning W3-2

A yield sign is ahead. Slow down and be ready to yield or stop.

Be Prepared to Stop

Warning W3-4

Traffic ahead may be stopped. Slow down and be ready to stop.

Speed Reduction Ahead

Warning W3-5

A lower speed limit is ahead. Begin slowing down.

Hairpin Curve

Warning W1-11

A very sharp, hairpin (near-U-turn) curve is ahead. Slow down sharply.

Uneven Lanes

Warning W8-11

The lanes ahead are at different levels (uneven lanes), usually in a work area. Keep a firm grip and drive carefully.

No Center Line

Warning W8-12

No center line on the road ahead. The pavement has no painted center line — keep well to the right and be careful when meeting or passing traffic.

Bicycle Crossing

Warning W11-1

Bicycles cross the road ahead. Slow down and watch for cyclists.

Deer Crossing

Warning W11-3

Deer may cross the road ahead, especially at dawn and dusk. Slow down and scan both sides.

Farm Vehicle Crossing

Warning W11-5

Farm vehicles (tractors and other slow machinery) cross or enter the road ahead. Slow down.

Dead End

Warning W14-1

The road ahead ends — it does not go through.

No Outlet

Warning W14-2

This street or area has only one way in and out — the road does not connect through.

Guide & information signs — 14 signs

Exit Only

Guide-Information E11-1

The lane with this panel above it must exit ahead — it does not continue through.

Interstate Route Marker

Guide-Information M1-1

Marks an Interstate highway route — it tells you the road you are on.

Business Route Marker

Guide-Information M1-2

Marks a designated business route — a highway branch that runs through the business part of a city.

Distance Sign

Guide-Information D2-1

Tells you the places ahead (cities, airports, and similar destinations) and how far away they are.

Milepost

Guide-Information D10-1

A small marker posted every mile along the highway. Use its number to tell responders where you are after a crash or breakdown.

Texas Loop Marker

Guide-Information TX-LOOP

Marks a Texas Loop — a short state highway route in or around a city or urban area. The number identifies the loop.

Texas State Highway Marker

Guide-Information TX-SH

Marks a Texas State Highway. The number identifies the highway you are on.

US Route Marker

Guide-Information M1-4

Marks a US highway route — a numbered highway in the national US route system.

Farm to Market Road Marker

Guide-Information TX-FM

Marks a Texas Farm to Market (or Ranch to Market) road — part of the state system serving rural and farming areas.

Hospital

Guide-Information D9-2

A hospital is nearby, in the direction of the arrow.

Fuel

Guide-Information D9-7

Fuel (gasoline) is available nearby, in the direction of the arrow.

Food

Guide-Information D9-8

Food is available nearby, in the direction of the arrow.

Lodging

Guide-Information D9-9

Lodging is available nearby, in the direction of the arrow.

Evacuation Route

Guide-Information EM-1

Marks a designated emergency evacuation route — follow it (with the direction arrows) during a hurricane or other emergency evacuation.

Work-zone signs — 6 signs

Road Work Ahead

Work-zone W20-1

Road construction or maintenance work is ahead. Slow down and watch for workers and equipment.

Detour Ahead

Work-zone W20-2

A detour begins ahead — traffic will be routed around the closed section.

Road Closed Ahead

Work-zone W20-3

The road ahead is closed at the work area. Be prepared to detour or turn.

One Lane Road Ahead

Work-zone W20-4

Only one lane is open ahead through the work area. Be prepared to stop and take turns.

Lane Closed Ahead

Work-zone W20-5

One lane ahead is closed in the work area. Merge early into the open lane.

Flagger STOP/SLOW Paddle

Work-zone W20-8

The paddle a flag person (flagger) in a work zone uses to direct traffic — one face says STOP, the other SLOW. Obey the flagger's instructions: stop in your lane and do not proceed until the flagger tells you to.

Railroad signs — 5 signs

Railroad Advance Warning

Railroad W10-1

A railroad crossing is within a few hundred feet ahead. Slow down, look and listen, be prepared to stop, and never try to beat a train.

Crossbuck

Railroad R15-1

Marks a railroad grade crossing itself. Yield the right-of-way to trains. If more than one track crosses, a small sign under the crossbuck shows the number of tracks.

Number of Tracks

Railroad R15-2P

Plaque used with the crossbuck when more than one track crosses the road — check every track before crossing.

Railroad Emergency Notification Sign

Railroad I1-13

Blue-and-white sign at a railroad crossing showing the railroad's emergency phone number and the crossing's USDOT inventory number — use them to report a stalled vehicle or other emergency at the crossing.

Low Ground Clearance Crossing

Railroad W10-5

The railroad crossing ahead has a high profile — low-clearance vehicles may hang up on the tracks. Such vehicles should use another route.

School signs — 3 signs

School Crossing

School S1-1

You are near a school. Slow down and watch carefully for children crossing.

School Zone Speed Limit

School S5-1

The speed limit shown applies in this school zone when the yellow lights on the sign are flashing.

School Bus Stop Ahead

School S3-1

A school bus stop is ahead. Slow down and be ready to stop for the bus and for children.

Slow-moving vehicle emblem — 1 signs

Slow-Moving Vehicle Emblem

SMV SMV emblem

Orange-and-red triangle emblem displayed on the back of vehicles that travel at 25 mph or less, such as farm machinery and animal-drawn vehicles. Slow down well in advance. It may not be displayed on other vehicles or on stationary objects.
