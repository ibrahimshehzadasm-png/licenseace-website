Adding a Car to Your Bike Licence: Endorsement, Explained

You took one learner permit for motorcycle and car together, sat the test, and the motorcycle licence was made — but the car category never appeared on your card. It does not add itself. Adding it later is a proper DLIMS service called endorsement, and here is exactly how it works.

🧾 First, what “endorsement” actually means

An endorsement is a new category added to the driving licence you already hold — printed on the same card, with its own date. You do not get a second licence, and your motorcycle licence is not cancelled or replaced by a “car licence”. The card is simply reissued showing both categories.

This is the situation thousands of Punjab drivers are in: one learner application covered motorcycle + car, the motorcycle test was passed first, and the permanent licence was issued for the motorcycle only. Many assume the car category will appear on its own, or at renewal. It will not. Until the car category is endorsed, that card authorises you to ride a motorcycle — not to drive a car.

1️⃣ Step 1 — Sort out the learner for the car category

Every category needs its own test, and every test needs a learner permit for that category that is at least 42 days old. What you do next depends on your original learner:

If your original combined learner is still valid — a Punjab learner is valid for 180 days (about 6 months) — and it is already more than 42 days old, you can go straight to booking the car test. No new learner, no new wait.

If it has expired — the common case, because the bike licence, practice and life in between easily eat up six months — take a fresh learner for the car category only. Online on DLIMS or the Dastak app it costs Rs 500 per category, paid by PSID, and the permit appears in your account as soon as the payment clears. Then the 42-day clock starts again for the car category. (Applicants over 50 also need a medical certificate signed by a government doctor.)

2️⃣ Step 2 — Pass the car tests (only the car tests)

At the licensing centre you sit the tests for the new category only — nobody will re-test your motorcycle riding. For the car category that means the e-sign test (10 traffic-sign questions, 5 correct to pass) and the practical driving test in a car, on the centre’s track. The car test fee on the DLIMS fee structure is Rs 150.

Practise the signs before you go — the e-sign test is short, but it is a hard gate: fail it and the driving test does not happen that day. Our free Punjab practice test uses the same 10-question format.

3️⃣ Step 3 — Pay the endorsement and collect the new card

Once you pass, the endorsement is processed against your existing licence record. On the published DLIMS schedule the endorsement / upgradation fee is Rs 200, the car category licence fee after passing is Rs 1,350, and the courier / card fee is Rs 480 — all paid through a PSID, never in cash at the counter. The exact total is whatever your generated PSID shows; if it differs from the figures here, the PSID is the one to trust.

A new card is then printed and delivered showing Motorcycle + Motor Car, each category with its own date, and your e-licence updates to match. Before you leave the centre — and again when the card arrives — check that both categories are actually printed. A missing category discovered months later means another round of visits.

💰 The full cost, both ways

Learner still valid: test fee Rs 150 + endorsement Rs 200 + licence fee Rs 1,350 + courier Rs 480 = about Rs 2,180.

Learner expired: add a fresh car learner at Rs 500 = about Rs 2,680, plus the 42-day wait.

Either way it is far cheaper than the alternative people drift into — driving a car on a motorcycle-only licence and collecting challans, or paying an agent to “manage” a process that is a single online application and one test day. Fees are taken from the DLIMS fee structure and FAQ as published in November 2026 and can change; confirm the amount on your PSID before paying.

⚠️ Three mistakes that make this harder than it is

Driving the car “because the learner covered it”. The learner covered practising, while it was valid. Once it expires, a motorcycle-only licence gives you no authority to drive a car at all — that is unlicensed driving, not a technicality.

Waiting for renewal. Renewal renews the categories already on the card. It never adds a new one. The car category only arrives through endorsement, with its own test.

Letting the second learner lapse too. If you take a fresh car learner, book the test as soon as the 42 days are up. A second expiry means a third learner fee — and a third wait.

Common questions

Can the car category be added without a test?

No. Every added category needs its own sign test and practical test. Holding a motorcycle licence — even for years — does not waive the car tests.

My combined learner expired after my bike licence was made. What exactly do I need?

A fresh learner for the car category only (Rs 500 online on DLIMS / Dastak), a 42-day wait, then the car e-sign and driving tests, then the endorsement payment. Your motorcycle category is untouched throughout.

Will I get a new card, or a stamp on the old one?

A new card. Endorsement reissues the licence showing all your categories together, and the e-licence in your DLIMS account is updated as well. Keep the old card until the new one is in your hand.

Can I add other categories the same way later — LTV, for example?

Yes, the route is the same: learner for that category, the wait, the tests, endorsement. Commercial categories add their own conditions — LTV starts at age 21 — so check the age and experience rules for the category before applying.
