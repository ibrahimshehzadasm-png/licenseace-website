Regulatory signs — 62 signs

Stop

Regulatory R1-1

Come to a complete stop at the limit line, crosswalk, or before the intersection; proceed only when safe.

Yield

Regulatory R1-2

Slow down and be ready to stop; let vehicles, bicyclists, and pedestrians pass before you go.

Do Not Enter

Regulatory R5-1

Do not enter the road or ramp where this sign is posted; traffic is coming toward you.

Wrong Way

Regulatory R5-1a

You are driving against traffic. When safe, pull over, stop, and turn around or back out.

No U-Turn

Regulatory R3-4

U-turns are not allowed at this location.

No Left Turn

Regulatory R3-2

Left turns are not allowed at this intersection.

No Right Turn

Regulatory R3-1

Right turns are not allowed at this intersection.

Two Way Traffic Ahead

Regulatory W44A (CA)

Two-way traffic begins or continues ahead; stay on your side of the road.

No Parking Any Time

Regulatory R28 (CA)

Parking is not allowed here at any time.

Yield to Uphill Traffic

Regulatory R55 (CA)

On a steep narrow road, downhill traffic must yield to uphill traffic (the downhill driver backs up if needed).

One Way

Regulatory R6-1 / R6-2

Traffic on this road moves in the direction of the arrow only. Do not drive the opposite way.

Emergency Parking Only

Regulatory R8-4

This space or shoulder may be used only for emergency parking.

No Turns

Regulatory R3-3

No turns of any kind (left, right, or U-turn) are allowed here.

Left Turn Yield on Green

Regulatory R10-11

On a green ball signal you may turn left only after yielding to oncoming traffic and pedestrians.

Do Not Pass

Regulatory R4-1

Do not pass other vehicles in this area.

Left Turn Only (lane control)

Regulatory R3-5a

This lane must turn left; you cannot go straight or turn right from it.

Left Turn or U-Turn Only (lane control)

Regulatory R3-6

From this lane you may turn left or make a U-turn; you cannot go straight.

Do Not Block Intersection

Regulatory R10-7

Do not enter the intersection unless you can clear it completely; never block cross traffic.

Slower Traffic Keep Right

Regulatory R4-3

If you are driving slower than surrounding traffic, stay in the right lane.

Keep Right

Regulatory R4-7

Keep to the right of the divider, median, or obstruction marked by this sign.

Carpool / HOV Lane sign (Handbook: CARPOOLS 3 OR MORE ONLY)

Regulatory R3-40

This lane is reserved for carpools with at least the stated number of people per vehicle. Solo drivers may not use it.

HOV Lane sign (Handbook: LEFT LANE - HOV 2+ - WHEN METERED)

Regulatory R3-14a

Left lane is an HOV 2+ lane when the ramp/lane is metered; follow the stated occupancy rule.

Slower Traffic Use Turnouts

Regulatory -

Slower traffic must use the next turnout area to let faster vehicles pass.

Reserved Parking - Disabled (wheelchair symbol)

Regulatory R7-8

This parking space is reserved for people with a disabled placard or special license plate.

Diagonal Crossing OK

Regulatory -

At this intersection pedestrians may cross diagonally in any direction while the walk signal allows it.

No Turn on Red

Regulatory R10-20aP / R13A (CA)

You may not turn on a red light at this intersection; wait for green.

NEV Use Only

Regulatory -

This road or lane is reserved for Neighborhood Electric Vehicles (NEVs / low-speed vehicles).

All Way plaque

Regulatory R1-3P

Plaque under a STOP sign: the stop applies in all directions - every approach must stop.

Yield Here to Pedestrians

Regulatory R1-5

Yield to pedestrians here: stop or yield at the marked line before the crosswalk.

In-Street Pedestrian Crossing

Regulatory R1-6

In-street sign at a crosswalk: state law - yield to pedestrians within the crosswalk.

State Law - Yield to Pedestrians Within Crosswalk

Regulatory R1-9

State law: yield to pedestrians within the crosswalk.

Combined Speed Limit

Regulatory R2-4a

One sign showing both the maximum and minimum speed limits for this road.

Begin Double Fine Zone

Regulatory R2-10

A double-fine zone begins: traffic fines are doubled here (usually near schools or work zones).

End Double Fine Zone

Regulatory R2-11

The double-fine zone ends here.

Mandatory Movement Lane Control

Regulatory R3-5

Lane-control sign: each lane may only make the movement shown (ONLY arrows).

Right Lane Must Turn Right

Regulatory R3-7

The right lane must turn right; it cannot go straight.

Center Lane Only - Two-Way Left-Turn Lane

Regulatory R3-9a

The center lane is a two-way left-turn lane only, shared by traffic from both directions; never use it for passing or travel.

No Straight Through

Regulatory R3-27

Driving straight through is prohibited at this intersection.

No Trucks

Regulatory R5-2

Trucks are not allowed on this road.

No Motor Vehicles

Regulatory R5-3

No motor vehicles of any kind are allowed beyond this sign.

No Pedestrians

Regulatory R5-6

Pedestrians are not allowed on this road (for example, a freeway).

No Bicycles

Regulatory R5-10c

Bicycles are not allowed on this road.

Divided Highway Crossing

Regulatory R6-3

A divided highway crosses your road; the sign shows which parts you may cross or turn into.

Roundabout Directional

Regulatory R6-4

Roundabout direction sign: traffic in the roundabout circulates counterclockwise, as the arrows show.

Bike Lane

Regulatory R7-9

This lane is a bicycle lane reserved for bicyclists.

No Pedestrian Crossing

Regulatory R9-3

Pedestrians may not cross here; use a crosswalk instead.

Stop Here on Red

Regulatory R10-6

Stop at this sign/line when the signal is red.

Turning Vehicles Yield to Pedestrians

Regulatory R10-15

Turning vehicles must yield to pedestrians crossing their path.

One Car per Green (ramp meter)

Regulatory R89 (CA)

Ramp meter rule: only one car may go on each green light.

Road Closed

Regulatory R11-2

The road is closed to all traffic. Do not drive around barricades.

Truck Route

Regulatory R14-1

This is a designated truck route.

HOV 2+ Only

Regulatory R3-11b

HOV lane: only vehicles with at least the stated number of occupants (2+) may use this lane.

HOV Lane Ends

Regulatory R3-12

The HOV lane restriction ends here; the lane becomes a general-purpose lane.

HOV - 2 or More Persons per Vehicle

Regulatory R3-40

HOV rule stated in words: this lane is for vehicles carrying 2 or more persons per vehicle.

Time-Limit Parking (CA)

Regulatory R32B (CA)

California parking sign: parking is limited to the stated time (example: 2 hours) during the hours shown.

No Parking - Street Sweeping (CA)

Regulatory R30B (CA)

California parking sign: no parking during the hours shown because of street sweeping (example legend).

Daylight Headlight Section (CA)

Regulatory S30-1 (CA)

Daylight headlight section begins: California requires headlights on in daylight on this stretch of road.

Fine for Littering - $1,000 (CA)

Regulatory R47 (CA)

California sign: littering carries a $1,000 fine.

Chains Required (CA)

Regulatory R76 (CA)

California mountain sign: tire chains are required from this point.

Move Over or Slow for Stopped Emergency Vehicles (CA)

Regulatory R110 (CA)

California "Move Over" sign: move over a lane or slow down for stopped emergency and maintenance vehicles.

Keep Left

Regulatory R4-8

Keep to the left of the divider or obstruction (used where traffic must pass on the left).

Stay in Lane

Regulatory R4-9

Stay in your lane; do not change lanes here.

Be Prepared to Stop

Warning W5-3

Warning W7-4

Bump

Warning W8-1

Dip

Warning W8-2

Pavement Ends

Warning W8-3

Low Shoulder

Warning W8-9

Uneven Lanes

Warning W8-11

Lanes ahead are uneven (different heights, common in resurfacing); drive carefully, especially on a motorcycle.

Shoulder Drop-Off

Warning W8-17

The edge of the pavement drops off sharply to the shoulder ahead. Avoid dropping a wheel off the edge.

Rough Road

Warning W8-21

The road surface ahead is rough. Slow down.

Low Ground Clearance Grade Crossing

Warning W10-5

The railroad crossing ahead has low ground clearance; long or low vehicles may get stuck on the tracks.

No Gates or Lights (railroad crossing)

Warning W10-9

The railroad crossing ahead has no gates or flashing lights. Look and listen carefully for trains.

Deer Crossing

Warning W11-3

Deer may cross the road ahead, especially at dawn and dusk. Slow down and scan the edges.

Cattle Crossing

Warning W11-4

Cattle may be on or crossing the road ahead (open range). Slow down and be ready to stop.

Tractor Crossing

Warning W11-5a

Tractors or farm vehicles may cross or enter the road ahead.

Equestrian Crossing

Warning W11-7

Horse riders may cross the road ahead. Slow down and do not startle the horses.

Fire Station

Warning W11-8

A fire station is ahead; fire trucks may enter the road suddenly.

Handicapped Crossing

Warning W11-9

People with disabilities may be crossing here. Slow down and yield.

Playground

Warning W11-15

A playground is nearby; children may run into the road. Slow down and watch carefully.

Bear Crossing

Warning W11-16

Bears may cross the road ahead (mountain and forest areas of California).

Low Clearance

Warning W12-1

The bridge or overpass ahead has the low clearance shown; tall vehicles must not pass under it.

Dead End

Warning W14-1

The road ends ahead with no outlet - a dead end.

No Outlet

Warning W14-2

This street or neighborhood has only one entrance and exit - no outlet.

No Passing Zone (pennant)

Warning W14-3

Yellow pennant sign: a no-passing zone begins here. Do not pass.

Speed Hump

Warning W17-1

A speed hump is in the road ahead. Slow down to cross it.

Freeway Ends

Warning W19-3

The freeway ends ahead; traffic moves onto a surface road.

Warning W38 (CA)

California sign: a slide area - rocks or earth may slide onto the road.

Warning W87 (CA)
