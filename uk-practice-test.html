Discover the LicenseAce app

Questions are written from The Highway Code in our own words — they are not the official DVSA test questions. The real theory test has 50 multiple-choice questions (pass mark 43) plus hazard perception (pass mark 44 out of 75), taken in one sitting; this 40-question set is for full practice.

About this UK practice test

This free practice test is built from LicenseAce's UK question bank — 730 original questions written by our team and checked against the DVSA syllabus and the Highway Code. They are practice questions written to the real test's syllabus and style, not copies of the official exam, and every answer carries a short explanation, so you learn the rule behind it rather than memorising a letter.

How to prepare with it

Work through the questions in short daily sessions, and read the explanation for every mistake — the questions you get wrong are the ones the real test is most likely to catch you on. When your scores are consistently strong, move to our traffic signs page and learn every sign you still hesitate on, then read the fees and guide page so the process, the cost and the documents hold no surprises on the day.
