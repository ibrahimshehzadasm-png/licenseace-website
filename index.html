What is LicenseAce?

LicenseAce is a free driving licence test preparation app. One app, many countries — each country and region has its own pack with its own question bank, traffic signs and driving guides, prepared from the official handbook in our own words.

Inside the app

❓

Verified question bank

400–500 practice questions per region, written from the official handbook — never copied word-for-word.

⏱️

Real mock tests

Timed tests in the real exam format, with results, a mistakes notebook and progress tracking.

🛑

Complete traffic signs

Every sign in your region's pack, with search and filters so you can revise fast.

🅿️

Parking & reverse guides

Step-by-step parallel parking, reverse bay parking, three-point turns and roundabouts — matched to your country's driving side.

🌍

Your language, side by side

Every question appears in the official language with a translation in the language you choose — made for expats and new residents.

🤝

Free, with fair ads

The app is free. Ads never appear during a test — only a few, capped, outside the exam.

Know your vehicle

Every driving licence starts with a vehicle category. Choose the one you drive — category names can vary slightly by country, but the idea is the same everywhere. LicenseAce packs guide you category by category.

🏍️

Motorcycle

Two-wheelers — bikes and scooters. A separate, lighter category in most regions, usually the quickest licence to get.

🚗

Car & Jeep

Private cars and jeeps — the most common licence of all, and the one most learners start with.

🚐

LTV — Light Transport

Vans, pickups and small commercial vehicles used for goods and passengers within weight limits.

🚚

HTV — Heavy Transport

Trucks, trailers and heavy goods vehicles. Needs more experience and a stricter test.

🚌

PSV — Public Service

Buses and passenger-carrying vehicles for public transport — extra responsibility, extra checks.

🚜

Tractor

Agricultural tractors and farm machinery — its own category in farming regions like Punjab.
