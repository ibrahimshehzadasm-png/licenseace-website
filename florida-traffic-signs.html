Regulatory signs — 40 signs

Stop

Regulatory R1-1

Come to a complete stop at the limit line, crosswalk, or before the intersection; proceed only when safe.

All Way Stop

Regulatory R1-3P

Plaque under a STOP sign: the stop applies in all directions - every approach must stop.

Yield

Regulatory R1-2

Slow down and be ready to stop; let vehicles, bicyclists, and pedestrians pass before you go.

Do Not Enter

Regulatory R5-1

Do not enter the road or ramp where this sign is posted; traffic is coming toward you.

Wrong Way

Regulatory R5-1a

You are driving against traffic. When safe, pull over, stop, and turn around or back out.

No U-Turn

Regulatory R3-4

U-turns are not allowed at this location.

No Right Turn

Regulatory R3-1

Right turns are not allowed at this intersection.

No Left Turn

Regulatory R3-2

Left turns are not allowed at this intersection.

No Turn on Red

Regulatory R10-11

On a green ball signal you may turn left only after yielding to oncoming traffic and pedestrians.

No Turns

Regulatory R3-3

No turns of any kind (left, right, or U-turn) are allowed here.

Must Turn

Regulatory R3-5

Lane-control sign: each lane may only make the movement shown (ONLY arrows).

One Way

Regulatory R6-1

Traffic on this street moves in the direction of the arrow only.

Keep Right

Regulatory R4-7

Keep to the right of the divider, median, or obstruction marked by this sign.

Slower Traffic Keep Right

Regulatory R4-3

If you are driving slower than surrounding traffic, stay in the right lane.

Do Not Pass

Regulatory R4-1

Do not pass other vehicles in this area.

Speed Limit

Regulatory R2-1

The maximum speed in miles per hour under ideal conditions. The same limit applies day and night.

Exit Speed

Regulatory W13-3

The highest safe speed, in miles per hour, for the exit ramp ahead.

Speeding Fines Doubled

Regulatory FL-SFD

Fines for speeding are doubled where this sign is posted — in active school zones and work zones. Slow down and obey the posted limit exactly.

Turn Lanes

Regulatory R3-5T

Lane-control sign: each lane may only make the movement shown (ONLY arrows).

Center Turn Lane

Regulatory R3-9a

The center lane is a two-way left-turn lane only, shared by traffic from both directions; never use it for passing or travel.

Left Lane Must Turn

Regulatory R3-6

Traffic in the left lane must turn left at the intersection. You cannot go straight or turn right from this lane.

Right Lane Must Turn

Regulatory R3-6R

Traffic in the right lane must turn right at the intersection. You cannot go straight or turn left from this lane.

Begin Right Turn Lane — Yield to Bicycles

Regulatory R4-4

A lane for right turns begins ahead. Drivers turning right should move into it; through traffic should stay out. Yield to bicyclists who may be crossing or using the lane.

Pass with Care

Regulatory R4-2

You have reached the end of a no-passing zone. Passing is permitted again, but only when it is safe and legal to do so.

Restricted Lane Ahead

Regulatory FL-RLA

A lane ahead is reserved for special users only — such as buses, carpools or bicycles. Do not enter it unless your vehicle qualifies.

Emergency Stopping Only

Regulatory FL-ESO

Stopping on this road is allowed only in an emergency. For any other stop, leave the roadway completely and use a safe place off the pavement.

No Parking on Pavement

Regulatory FL-NPP

Parking on the paved part of the road is prohibited. If you must stop, pull completely off the pavement onto the shoulder or grass where legal.

Parking by Disabled Permit Only

Regulatory R7-8FL

This space is reserved for vehicles displaying an official disabled parking permit. In Florida, misuse is punished with a penalty fine — the field assembly carries the state penalty plaque below the sign.

No Parking

Regulatory R8-3

Parking is prohibited here (symbol sign).

No Stopping

Regulatory FL-NST

Stopping is prohibited here at all times — even for a moment to drop off or pick up passengers.

No Standing

Regulatory FL-NSA

Standing is prohibited here: you may not wait or load in this space. A brief stop to obey traffic or avoid danger is still allowed.

No Trucks

Regulatory R5-2

Trucks are not allowed on this road.

No Bicycles

Regulatory R5-10c

Bicycles are not allowed on this road.

No Pedestrians

Regulatory R5-6

Pedestrians are not allowed on this road (for example, a freeway).

No Motor Vehicles

Regulatory R5-3

No motor vehicles of any kind are allowed beyond this sign.

Weight Limit

Regulatory R12-5

Weight limit. Vehicles (or loads) over the posted weight — 10 tons on this example sign — may not use this road or bridge.

Keep Left

Regulatory R4-8

Keep to the left of the divider or obstruction (used where traffic must pass on the left).

HOV / Carpool Lane

Regulatory R3-40

This lane is reserved for carpools with at least the stated number of people per vehicle. Solo drivers may not use it.

Turning Vehicles Yield to Pedestrians

Regulatory R10-15

Turning vehicles must yield to pedestrians crossing their path.

Stop Here on Red Signal

Regulatory R10-6

Stop at this sign/line when the signal is red.

Warning signs — 50 signs

Traffic Signal Ahead

Warning W3-3

A traffic signal is ahead. Be ready to stop.

Stop Sign Ahead

Warning W3-1

A STOP sign is ahead. Slow down and be ready to stop.

Yield Ahead

Warning W3-2

A YIELD sign is ahead. Slow down and be ready to yield or stop.

Speed Reduction Ahead

Warning W3-5

A lower speed limit is ahead; begin slowing to the new limit.

Advisory Speed

Warning W13-1

The highest safe speed for the curve or condition ahead, in miles per hour. It advises — slow to this speed.

Two-Way Traffic Ahead

Warning W6-3

You are entering or continuing on a road with traffic coming from the opposite direction on the same roadway.

Merging Traffic

Warning W4-1

Traffic is merging into your lane ahead. Adjust speed and leave space for merging vehicles.

Lane Ends — Merge Left

Warning W4-2

Your lane ends ahead. Merge safely into the next lane.

Roundabout Ahead

Warning W2-6

A circular intersection (roundabout) is ahead. Slow down and yield inside the circle.

Divided Highway Ahead

Warning W6-1

The road ahead splits into a divided highway with a median or barrier.

Divided Highway Ends

Warning W6-2

The divided highway ends ahead; traffic will share one roadway again.

Right Curve

Warning W1-2

A curve is ahead. Slow down before you enter it.

Sharp Right Turn

Warning W1-1

A sharp turn is ahead in the direction of the arrow. Slow down.

Reverse Curve

Warning W1-4

Two curves in opposite directions are ahead (reverse curve). Slow down.

Winding Road

Warning W1-5

A series of curves and turns is ahead. Slow down and keep your lane.

Cross Road

Warning W2-1

A road crosses your road ahead. Watch for traffic entering from the side.

Side Road

Warning W2-2

A side road enters your road ahead at an angle. Watch for entering traffic.

Bicycle Crossing

Warning W11-1

Bicycles cross the road ahead. Slow down and watch for cyclists.

Emergency Vehicle Crossing

Warning W11-8

Emergency vehicles — such as fire trucks or ambulances — may enter or cross the road here, near a fire station or emergency facility. Be ready to stop and yield.

Truck Crossing

Warning W8-6

Trucks cross or enter the road ahead. Slow down and watch for large vehicles.

Pedestrian Crossing

Warning W11-2

Pedestrians may cross here. Slow down, yield to people crossing, and be ready to stop.

Animal Crossing (Deer)

Warning W11-3

Deer may cross the road ahead, especially at dawn and dusk. Slow down and scan the edges.

Slippery When Wet

Warning W8-5

The road may be slippery when wet. Slow down and avoid sudden moves, especially in the first rain.

Dip

Warning W8-2

A dip in the road is ahead. Slow down.

Narrow Bridge

Warning W5-2

A narrow bridge is ahead; slow down and watch for oncoming traffic.

One Lane Bridge

Warning W5-3

A one-lane bridge is ahead; yield to traffic already on or approaching the bridge.

Hill / Downgrade

Warning W7-1

A steep hill (downgrade) is ahead. Use a lower gear and control your speed.

Low Clearance

Warning W12-2

The overpass or structure ahead has the vertical clearance shown. Do not enter if your load is higher.

Pavement Ends

Warning W8-3

The paved surface ends ahead; the road continues as gravel or dirt.

Soft Shoulder

Warning W8-4

The shoulder is soft and may not support a vehicle; stay on the pavement.

Left Curve

Warning W1-2L

The road curves to the left ahead. Slow down before the curve and keep your vehicle in its lane.

Hairpin Curve

Warning W1-11

A hairpin (very sharp, near-U-turn) curve is ahead. Slow down sharply.

Chevron Alignment

Warning W1-8

Chevron signs mark the edge of a sharp curve or turn; follow the road direction they show.

Large Arrow

Warning W1-6

The road turns sharply in the direction of the arrow; this large arrow sign marks the turn.

Double Arrow

Warning W1-7

The road ends or is blocked ahead at an island or obstruction, but you may pass on either side of it.

T-Intersection

Warning W2-4

Your road ends at a T intersection ahead; you must turn left or right.

Y-Intersection

Warning W2-5

A Y-shaped intersection is ahead; the road forks.

Added Lane

Warning W4-3

An added lane joins from the right ahead; entering traffic does not have to merge immediately.

Road Narrows

Warning W5-1

The pavement narrows ahead. Slow down and stay in your lane.

Bump

Warning W8-1

A bump in the road is ahead. Slow down.

Loose Gravel

Warning W8-7

Loose gravel covers the road ahead; slow down to avoid skidding and flying stones.

Shoulder Drop-Off

Warning W8-17

The edge of the pavement drops off sharply to the shoulder ahead. Avoid dropping a wheel off the edge.

Falling Rocks

Warning W8-14

Rocks may fall onto the road in this area, or fallen rocks may be lying on the pavement. Be alert, watch the road ahead, and be ready to slow down or stop.

Dead End

Warning W14-1

The road ends ahead with no outlet - a dead end.

No Passing Zone

Warning W14-3

Yellow pennant sign: a no-passing zone begins here. Do not pass.

Drawbridge Ahead

Warning W3-6

A drawbridge is ahead; be ready to stop when it opens for boats.

Playground Ahead

Warning W11-15

A playground is nearby; children may run into the road. Slow down and watch carefully.

Fire Station Ahead

Warning W11-8F

A fire station is ahead; fire trucks may enter the road suddenly.

Truck Rollover Warning

Warning W1-13

This stretch of road has a history of trucks rolling over — usually because of speed on a curve. Truck drivers must slow down well before the curve.

Golf Cart Crossing

Warning FL-GCC

Golf carts may cross the road here — a common Florida crossing near golf courses and golf-cart communities. Slow down and watch both ways for carts entering the road.

Guide & information signs — 8 signs

Interstate Route Marker

Guide-Information M1-1

Red, white, and blue shield: an Interstate highway route marker.

U.S. Route Marker

Guide-Information M1-4

Marks a US highway route — a numbered highway in the national US route system.

Florida State Road Marker

Guide-Information FL-SR

This marker identifies a Florida State Road. State roads are numbered routes maintained by the state; the marker helps you confirm you are on the right route.

County Route Marker

Guide-Information M1-6

County route marker: identifies a signed county route.

Destination and Distance

Guide-Information D2-1

Green distance sign: distances to the named places ahead.

Junction

Guide-Information M2-1

Junction plaque. Used above a route marker where two numbered routes meet or cross ahead.

Exit Number Plaque

Guide-Information E5-1

This plaque shows the number of the freeway exit ahead, so you can plan your exit and be in the correct lane in good time.

Hurricane Evacuation Route

Guide-Information EM-1

Emergency management route sign: follow this route during an evacuation or emergency.

Gas Station

Service D9-2

Fuel (gas) is available in the direction shown. Blue service signs lead to the fuel station.

Food / Restaurant

Service D9-8

Food is available nearby, in the direction of the arrow.

Lodging

Service D9-9

Lodging is available nearby, in the direction of the arrow.

Hospital

Service D9-1

Hospital services are available in the direction shown. Follow the blue service signs to reach the hospital.

Rest Area

Service D5-1

A highway rest area is ahead (Handbook example: REST AREA 1 MILE).

Work-zone signs — 5 signs

Road Work Ahead

Work-zone W20-1

Road work is ahead. Slow down, watch for workers, and be ready for lane changes. Traffic fines are doubled in work zones.

Flagger Ahead

Work-zone W20-7

Flagger ahead in a work area. Slow down and obey the flag person’s signals (STOP, SLOW or PROCEED paddles).

Workers Ahead

Work-zone W21-1

Workers are on or near the road ahead. Slow down and give them room.

Lane Closed Ahead

Work-zone W20-5

A lane is closed ahead (symbol sign with flagger-style arrow board pattern). Merge early.

Detour Ahead

Work-zone W20-2

A detour begins ahead because of road work; follow the detour signs.

Railroad signs — 5 signs

Railroad Advance Warning

Railroad W10-1

A railroad crossing is ahead (round yellow sign). Slow down, look, listen, and be ready to stop.

Railroad Crossbuck

Railroad R15-1

The crossbuck (X-shaped RAILROAD CROSSING blades) marks the crossing itself. Yield to trains; never stop on the tracks.

Number of Tracks Plaque

Railroad R15-2P

Plaque under the crossbuck showing how many tracks the crossing has. Check every track before crossing.

Railroad Flashing Light Signal

Railroad FL-RRFL

Flashing red lights at a railroad crossing mean a train is approaching or passing. Stop at least 15 feet from the tracks and do not go until the lights stop flashing and it is safe.

Railroad Emergency Notification Sign

Railroad I1-13

Blue sign at railroad crossings: call the number shown to report a problem or emergency on the tracks; the X-ING number identifies the crossing.

School signs — 6 signs

School Sign

School S1-1

You are near a school. Drive slowly, watch for children, and stop for children in the crosswalk.

School Crossing

School W15-1

School crossing ahead (pentagon-shaped assembly in school colors): slow down and yield to children crossing.

Begin School Zone

School FL-SBZ

A school zone begins here. From this point the school speed limit (20 mph in Florida) applies at the posted times or when the lights are flashing, and speeding fines are doubled.

End School Zone

School S5-2

The school zone has ended. The regular posted speed limit applies again from this point — but keep watching for children near the road.

School Bus Stop Ahead

School S3-1

A school bus stop is ahead; watch for stopped buses and children.

School Speed Limit Assembly

School FL-SSL

Inside a school zone the speed limit is 20 mph when children are present, at the posted times, or when the beacons are flashing. Fines for speeding here are doubled.

Slow-moving vehicle emblem — 1 signs

Slow-Moving Vehicle Emblem

SMV SMV-emblem

Orange-and-red triangle emblem on the back of a vehicle traveling at 25 mph or less (farm equipment, etc.). Slow down well in advance.
