Privacy Policy — LicenseAce

Effective date: 4 November 2026
Developer: AR&A Developers ("we", "us")
Contact: ibrahimshehzad.asm@gmail.com

1. Overview

LicenseAce is a free driving licence test preparation app. We respect your privacy. The app is designed to work without an account and without collecting your name, phone number or other directly identifying information.

2. Information the app itself collects

The app stores your study progress (test results, mistakes notebook, streaks and settings) on your own device only. This data is not sent to us and is removed when you uninstall the app.

3. Third-party services

The app uses the following third-party services, which may collect data as described in their own policies:

  • Google AdMob — to show advertisements. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID and general device information to serve and measure ads. See Google's Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy and how Google uses data from apps: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
  • Google Play Services — for core app functionality on Android devices.

Where required by law, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your device's settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).

4. How data is used

Data processed by the third-party services above is used only to operate the app, show and measure advertising, and prevent fraud and abuse. We do not sell your personal data.

5. Children

LicenseAce is intended for people who are preparing for a driving licence test and is not directed at children under 13 years of age.

6. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above.

7. Contact us

For any question about this policy, contact us at ibrahimshehzad.asm@gmail.com.
