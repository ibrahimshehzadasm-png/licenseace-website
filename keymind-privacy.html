Privacy Policy — KeyMind IQ

Effective date: 22 November 2026
Last updated: 22 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game KeyMind IQ ("the Game", "we", "us") handles information. KeyMind IQ is designed to respect your privacy: the Game has no accounts, no sign-up and no login, and everything you enter or achieve in the Game is stored only on your device.

By installing and using KeyMind IQ, you agree to this policy.

1. Information stored on your device only

When you first open the Game, you are asked for a name and age. While you play, the Game also saves your game progress — for example, levels completed, stars earned, Hint Keys, your rank, Daily Challenge results and streak, and your settings.

  • This information is stored locally on your device only, using your device's on-device storage.
  • It is never transmitted to us, never uploaded to a server, and never leaves your device through the Game itself.
  • We have no access to it, and we cannot see, recover or restore it for you.
  • If you uninstall the Game or clear the app's data, this information is permanently deleted from your device.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information.

Specifically, the developer of KeyMind IQ:

  • does not collect, receive, store, sell or share your name, age, or game progress;
  • does not operate any server that receives data from the Game;
  • does not require or offer user accounts;
  • does not use any third-party analytics, crash-reporting or tracking service of our own.

3. Advertising — Google Mobile Ads (AdMob)

The Game is supported by ads provided through Google Mobile Ads (AdMob), a service of Google LLC. This is the only third-party service integrated into the Game.

To serve, measure and (where you have allowed it) personalise ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process certain information from your device, which can include:

  • device identifiers, including the Android Advertising ID;
  • IP address, which may be used to derive approximate (coarse) location;
  • device information such as model, operating system version and language;
  • ad interaction data (for example, which ads were shown or clicked) and diagnostic/performance data.

This data is collected and processed by Google, acting under its own policies — not by us. We do not receive your personal data from Google; we receive only aggregated, non-identifying advertising reports (for example, total ad impressions and revenue).

You can learn more here:

  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • About AdMob: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

Your choices: You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalised advertising at any time in your device settings (for example, Settings → Privacy → Ads on Android, or via Google Settings). Where required by law, the Game will request your consent before serving personalised ads; if you decline, you will still see ads, but they will not be personalised. Ads are only loaded while your device is online; the Game itself works fully offline.

4. Sharing of information

We do not share your personal information with anyone, because we do not collect or hold any. The only data processing by a third party is the advertising processing by Google described in Section 3.

5. Children

KeyMind IQ is designed for an adult audience and is not directed at children — including children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). The Game is a general-audience puzzle game for grown-ups; it contains no features aimed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. The name and age entered at first launch are stored only on the device, as described in Section 1, and are never sent to us.

6. Data security and retention

Because your name, age and progress never leave your device, their security depends on your device's own security (such as your screen lock). The data is retained on your device for as long as the Game is installed and its app data is not cleared. Deleting the Game deletes the data.

7. Your rights

Since we hold no personal data about you, there is no data for us to access, correct, export or delete on our side. You can remove all Game data at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the Game. For data processed by Google through advertising, please see Google's Privacy Policy and the controls described in Section 3.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example if the Game's features or advertising arrangements change. Any updated version will be published at the same location as this policy, with a new "Last updated" date. Continued use of the Game after an update means you accept the revised policy.
